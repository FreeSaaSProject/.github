## Principles

These principles guide what we are building:

- **Open source** — freedom to use, study, modify, and share software under its open-source license.
- **Local first** — core functionality runs on your own computer or server.
- **Own your data** — keep control of your data, with open formats and portability.
- **Commodity hardware** — aim for practical deployment on readily available hardware, with requirements documented for each release.
- **No mandatory cloud** — cloud services are optional for core functionality.
